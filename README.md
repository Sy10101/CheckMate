# 🎲 Check<: Mate

> **"Find friendships that resonate with you!"**

**Check<: Mate** is an interactive web-based board game. It is designed based on MBTI and Jungian cognitive functions.
In an era of rapid technological advancement where people are becoming increasingly independent, we hope to offer a fun and engaging path for people to better understand themselves and others, and to explore diverse interpersonal relationships.
<br>

## 🎮 How to Play

1.  **Character Selection**: Click "Start!" and choose your MBTI type as your exclusive game piece. You can also use the search box in the top-right corner (supporting both uppercase and lowercase) and click "search" or press "Enter" to find it quickly.
2.  **8x8 Board Exploration**: Each cell presents a multiple-choice question based on daily life, friendship, and fantasies.
3.  **Light Beam Guidance**: Choosing different options determines the next direction of your piece, shown by a beam-like visual effect in the theme color of that personality type.
4.  **Dice of Fate**: Click the "roll" button on the right side to randomly determine how many steps to move in that direction.
5.  **Friendship Encounter**: When the piece reaches the top or right edge of the board but continues to move outwards, the game ends. The personality type by the cell you land on becomes the "friend" you have found, and a pop-up modal will present an introduction to that personality and what kind of friend they would be.
6.  **Relax Anytime**: The interface includes a menu button (to exit or return to character selection at any time) and a background music toggle for a comfortable gaming experience.
<br>

## 💡 Inspiration & Philosophy

The name **Check<: Mate** is a combination of the chess term "Checkmate" and the kaomoji smiley face `<:`:
*   **Check**: Represents "to search, verify, and confirm".
*   **Mate**: Represents "companion" or "partner".

**"Man proposes, God disposes."**: You can decide the direction your piece moves (just like your personal preferences in life), but the dice determine how far you go. The connections between people are wonderfully mysterious—influenced by our preferences, yet also shaped by the arrangements of fate.

**About MOMO**: If you don't know your MBTI type yet, you can choose the **"MOMO"** type listed first. This is a unique phenomenon in Chinese internet culture (anonymity, security, and speaking freely). In the game, MOMO represents a freer and more open-ended option beyond the 16 personality types.
<br>

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

*   **HTML5**: Builds the game framework and DOM structure.
*   **CSS3**: Aesthetic UI layout and beam effects.
*   **JavaScript**: Implements board logic, piece dynamics, random dice functionality, and music control.
<br>

## 🚀 How to Run

No dependencies required, just run it directly in your browser!
